# Articulate Daily — Customer Guide

## Build clearer English every day

Articulate Daily helps you collect useful vocabulary, understand it in context, practise it repeatedly and use it with greater confidence at work and in daily life.

## A simple daily routine

1. Open **Today** and listen to the word of the day.
2. Save words that matter to you rather than collecting everything.
3. Complete the short review, sentence and pronunciation targets.
4. Use one saved word in a real message, meeting or conversation.
5. Return tomorrow; small, repeated practice is more valuable than occasional long sessions.

## Main features

### Today

See your daily word, progress, review queue, weekly activity and personalised learning targets.

### Add a word

Enter a word or phrase, look up a public dictionary definition, add your own example, and organise it with a category, tags, notes and difficulty.

### Extract words

Choose a PDF, DOCX, image, text, Markdown, CSV or JSON file. Processing happens on your device. Review the suggestions and save only the vocabulary you want to practise.

### Practice

- Write the target word in a complete sentence.
- Rate recall as Again, Hard, Good or Easy.
- Learn collocations, word families and distinctions.
- Test meanings, phrases and gap-fill questions.
- Listen to UK English speech and compare what speech recognition heard.

The recognition percentage is a transcript match, not a clinical or phonetic assessment.

### Speaking Coach

Use structured prompts for meetings, delays, data explanations, presentations, interviews and polite disagreement. The coach is rule-based and helps frame practice responses; it is not a live factual or company-information service.

### Library

Search, filter, favourite, review, pronounce, master or delete saved vocabulary.

### Profiles and Settings

- Create separate local profiles.
- Change theme and text size.
- Set realistic daily learning targets.
- Export or import a complete JSON backup.
- Move vocabulary through CSV.
- Download a weekly HTML report.
- Optionally sign in for cloud sync.

## Using the app effectively

- Prefer words connected to your real work, study or conversations.
- Write examples that describe your own situations.
- Say a full sentence aloud instead of repeating an isolated word.
- Review difficult words sooner and familiar words less often.
- Add notes explaining where you heard a word and when you want to use it.
- Download a backup regularly, especially before importing or deleting data.

## Privacy and data

The app saves data on your device by default. Optional cloud sync stores the same app state in your authenticated Supabase account. Dictionary lookup sends only the requested word to a public dictionary service. Selected documents are processed on the device by bundled browser libraries. Browser speech recognition may use an online service provided by the browser vendor.

Never store passwords, confidential company records, medical information or other sensitive personal information in vocabulary notes or uploaded practice materials.

## Browser support

Use a current version of Chrome or Edge for the broadest voice-recognition support. Most non-voice features also work in current Safari and Firefox versions. Microphone features require HTTPS in production.

## Backup and recovery

Open **Profiles & backup**, choose **Download backup**, and store the JSON file somewhere safe. To restore it, choose **Import backup**, inspect the confirmation, and continue only when you intend to replace the current app data.
