Data and privacy

Words, profiles and progress are stored in this browser. If you enable cloud sync, the same app data is also stored in your Supabase account. Clearing browser data can remove the local copy, so keep a backup. Dictionary lookup sends the requested word to a public dictionary service. Selected documents are processed in your browser; browser speech recognition may use your browser provider's online speech service.

Before deleting: download a backup if you may want this information again. Deletion requires two confirmations.
